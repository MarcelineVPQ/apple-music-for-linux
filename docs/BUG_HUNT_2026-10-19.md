## The flaky test

Reported: one test failed once in a full `npm test` on 2026-10-19 and passed on a rerun. Not reproduced: 5 full `npm test` runs (988/988 each, ~60 s) on a separate checkout of bd85dea, plus 15 runs each of the timing-sensitive files (lookahead, sheet-notes, sheet-notes-given, notes-log, align-session, align-helper, expressive, helper-install, align-install, lyrics-race): 0 failures. A CPU-load reproduction was not run (it would compete with Marcy's playing Sonata).

Most likely culprit, by margin: `test/lookahead.test.js` "controller: a gate the player never confirms is opened after 100 ms, not 3 s" waits a fixed 130 ms for a 100 ms timer: 30 ms of slack, while `node --test` runs ~90 files in parallel processes. A busy machine (an align job, a build) easily delays a timer 30 ms. Next by margin: the fixed `settle()` waits of 10-15 ms in `test/sheet-notes.test.js`, `test/sheet-notes-given.test.js` and `test/notes-log.test.js`, and the 350 ms waits in the two lookahead tests after it. Fix: wait for the condition (the `until(cond, ms)` helper other tests use) instead of a fixed sleep, or inject the controller's clock.
