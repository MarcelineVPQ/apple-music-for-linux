What to judge

  1. The melody lane: the +1's pitch line, karaoke style. Bars are the tune, the bright line is the voice, drawn as it's heard.
  2. Singers keep their own colour and side (Ada left, Ben right, together centre); the legend lights up for whoever sings.
  3. The sung line, big, filling word by word (a wipe, compositor-only); the next line waits underneath with its singer's name.
  4. Countdown dots before an entry (the intro, and the fiddle break at 1:00): on the coming singer's side, one per beat.
  5. The bottom bar: verses and choruses as sections in the singers' colours.
