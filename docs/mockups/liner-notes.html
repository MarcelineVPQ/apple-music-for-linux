What to judge

  1. The booklet metaphor: square CD pages, a spread at a time, the page turning on the spine. Click the arrows, the section buttons, or press ← →.
  2. Cover → tray → notes → venue → credits → lyrics → back cover. Is that the order you'd read a real booklet in?
  3. Every block carries its source chip (Apple, setlist.fm, MusicBrainz, Discogs, Wikipedia + licence). A section with no real data just isn't printed.
  4. The Wikipedia page is the real article text (CC BY-SA, trimmed, linked), matched through Wikidata, never a title guess.
  5. The lyrics pages follow the song: the line being sung is marked as it plays (press play).
  6. Paper = the theme's surface: try Light, and the album colours.
